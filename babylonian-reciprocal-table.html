Only regular numbers — numbers built purely from the primes 2, 3, and 5 — appear as p. Those are the only ones whose partner terminates cleanly in base 60, exactly like the reciprocal tables Babylonian scribes carved into clay; a number like 7 or 11 would produce an endlessly repeating fraction. A comma separates the whole part from the sexagesimal fraction: 7, 30 means 7 and 30/60 (7.5); 2, 13, 20 means 2 + 13/60 + 20/3600.
